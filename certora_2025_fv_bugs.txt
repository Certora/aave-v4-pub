HOW TO GET SPECIFIC CVL PROPERTIES
================================================================================

To see the actual CVL rules/properties that caught bugs:
1. Download the full PDF reports from https://www.certora.com/reports
2. Look for sections titled:
   - "Formal Verification"
   - "Verified Properties"
   - "Specification"
   - "Counterexamples Found"

Example of what a CVL property looks like:

    rule noFundLoss(address user) {
        uint256 balanceBefore = token.balanceOf(user);
        // ... operation ...
        uint256 balanceAfter = token.balanceOf(user);
        assert balanceAfter >= balanceBefore;
    }
